# How to Edit the Website (for Dru & Ashley)

You can change the website's programs, schedule, belt roster, and membership
pricing yourself — no coding needed.

## Logging in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**
2. Sign in with the **email address** your invite was sent to (check your inbox
   for the invitation the first time).
3. Open **evolution-martial-arts-nl**. You'll see four sections in the sidebar:
   - **Programs** — the five program cards on the site
   - **Weekly Schedule** — days, class times, class names
   - **Membership Pricing** — the pricing cards
   - **Team & Belt Roster** — Dru's bio and the belt lists

Make your changes, then click **Save**. The site updates itself automatically —
**allow about 2 minutes**, then refresh the page (hold **Shift** while clicking
refresh to see the newest version).

## Common tasks

### Change a class time
1. Open **Weekly Schedule** and find the day.
2. Click the class to expand it, then pick a new **Start time** / **End time**
   from the dropdowns. Save.

### Add or remove a class
- **Add:** on the right day, click **Add entry**, fill in the class name and
  times. Save.
- **Remove:** open the class entry and use its **delete** (trash/…) button. Save.
- The optional **Note** field shows in brackets after the class name — e.g.
  "on break until September".

### Promote a member (belt roster)
1. Open **Team & Belt Roster**.
2. Find their name under their current belt and **delete that entry**.
3. Under the new belt, click **Add entry** and type their name. Save.

### Change a price
1. Open **Membership Pricing** and click the plan.
2. Edit **Price** — just the number, like `138` or `172.50` (no dollar sign).
3. **Billing period** is the text after the price, usually `/month`. Save.

### Update Dru's bio
Open **Team & Belt Roster** → **Lead Instructor** → edit **Bio**. Save.

## Good to know

- **Red error under a field?** Fix that field — the message says what's wrong.
  Your change won't publish until it's fixed.
- If you save something the site can't use, the site simply keeps showing the
  previous version (it never breaks), and Michael gets notified automatically.
- Best to have **one person editing a section at a time** — if you both save
  the same section, the last save wins.
- Photos and anything not listed above: text Michael.
- Anything looks wrong on the site after a change? Text Michael.
